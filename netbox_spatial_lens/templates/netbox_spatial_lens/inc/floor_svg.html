{% load lens %} {% comment %} The floor itself. One SVG in room coordinates: the viewBox is the room in centimetres plus a margin for the scale, so every rack is drawn at its true size and the browser does the scaling. Nothing here computes pixels, which is what lets the same markup serve the read-only view and the editor. Each rack is a translated to its centre and then rotated, so a rotation turns the rack where it stands instead of swinging it around a corner. The shapes inside are drawn from half a footprint back, which is what offset_x and offset_y are. Identity and geometry ride on data attributes. The editor reads and writes those, and never re-parses the page. {% endcomment %}
